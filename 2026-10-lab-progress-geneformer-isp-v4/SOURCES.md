# Sources for every number in "Evaluating in-silico perturbation with Geneformer" (version 4)

Version 4 is version 3 (talks `main` at `a52428c`) plus a third bulk panel (slide 29, K562 CRISPRi, added 3 October 2026) and a part on transposable elements, slides 30 to 36, whose sources are listed in their own sections below; slides 1 to 28 keep their version 3 sources, slides 37 to 39, 41 and 42 are version 3 slides 29 to 33, and slide 40 is a cross-project synthesis. Rows in the version 3 sections that cite a later slide use the version 4 numbers. Version 3 reuses the numbers of versions 1 and 2 (`../2026-10-lab-progress-geneformer-isp/` and `../2026-10-lab-progress-geneformer-isp-v2/`) and adds figures drawn from existing results: the external-cohort feasibility count (E0), the colon classifier gate (E2) and the bulk perturbation test against measured knockouts (slides 27-28). It adds no new measurement of its own. Files under `source/data/` are ten inputs: the five result files of version 2 (three byte-identical to the result files named below; in the two null-study files the free-text `about` notes were shortened for publication and every value is unchanged) the E0 per-donor count table (byte-identical, sha256 `3e47f81b0cd2f44d73d47e88650fd4ade809775bcd4f073a3b5dfb5b4086f2e9`) and four bulk-test tables (see slides 26 to 28). Version 4 adds five fish tables, six zebrafish tables, four Freimer TE-arm tables (slides 30 to 36), three colon result files (slide 24) and three K562 tables (slide 29), so `source/data/` holds thirty-one files. `source/SHA256SUMS` lists the copies' hashes.
